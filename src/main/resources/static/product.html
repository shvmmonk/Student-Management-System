Product Details

Product Name

Student Management System

Description

A simple web application for managing student information. It allows academic staff and administrators to handle records effortlessly through an intuitive interface.

Purpose

To provide educational institutions with an efficient, reliable, and user-friendly digital system for recording, updating, and tracking student profiles and academic activities without cumbersome paper documentation.

Specifications & Audience

Technologies Used

Target Users

🎓 Students 👨‍🏫 Teachers 🏫 Colleges

Designed for students wishing to view academic information, teachers managing course progress, and colleges overseeing entire departmental databases.
